Modified versions supporting remote network interaction must offer their Corresponding Source to interacting users as required by section 13.

Preserve the original-project attribution **Lumina-Layers (Lumina Studio)** and project address **https://github.com/lumina-layer-studio/Lumina-Layers** in source notices and the software interface, under AGPL section 7(b).

## Relicensing and history

The project owners have confirmed that all relevant rights holders have authorized AGPLv3 licensing and separate commercial licensing of their current and historical contributions. Copyright ownership remains unchanged.
