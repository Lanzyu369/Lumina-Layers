## Printer picker artwork and slicer icons

`machine_registry/ASSET_SOURCES.json` records the shipped image digests,
application versions, upstream matches and transformations. Registry images
were copied from official slicer profile resources. Large raster covers may
be resized by `tools/machine_adapter/import_profiles.py`; their artwork is
not redrawn. The 18 Prusa picker images are 512-pixel renders of official
PrusaResearch FFF 1.0.11 SVGs; their release-package provenance and hardware
mapping remain in
`machine_registry/slicers/PrusaSlicer/3.0.0-alpha12/SOURCES.md` and
`picker-assets.json`.

`third_party/interface-assets/sources.json` pins the inspected upstream source
revisions and original license digests. Slicer software versions in the
registry describe the imported application resources; they are not relabeled
as the newer inspected upstream source revision. Product and company names
and logos identify the corresponding application or printer; they remain
their respective owners' marks. No endorsement is implied.

Software icon extraction and source paths are recorded in
`frontend/src/assets/slicer-logos/SOURCES.md`. The former Flash Studio website
header crop is not covered by the inspected source-repository license and is
no longer bundled; that entry uses the existing generic slicer icon.

## Generated printer plates

The six neutral plate images are generated by Lumina's
`tools/machine_adapter/build_plates.py`; see
`machine_registry/BUILD_PLATE_SOURCES.md`. They are separate from third-party
printer artwork and from native bed-model filenames resolved at runtime.

## ChromaPrint3D data and native geometry

Six files under `assets/native_sources/ChromaPrint3D/data/` come from
<https://github.com/neroued/ChromaPrint3D> under Apache-2.0, copyright
2025 neroued. The model pack `model_packs/bambulab_pla.msgpack` is an unmodified
copy from `e0fd5402c47de674b054c0e832af320e9ca3f342`; the five JSON files are
unmodified copies from `72faa6f76d2ee852299a35fb1f3d9ac2a5472039`. Their complete
[Apache license](native/lumina-svg-core/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/ChromaPrint3D.txt)
is retained. No upstream NOTICE file was present at those inspected revisions.

The legacy local native SVG module and its source dependencies keep the
independent licenses and notices in
`native/lumina-svg-core/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. The optional local GEOS build
keeps its LGPL terms and modification notice in
`native/geos_buffer_fast/NOTICE.md`.

Installed `lumina-svg-core`, FatCat and native writer packages carry their
own licenses and source notices; the legacy local source module is not the
installed `lumina-svg-core==0.3.4` distribution.
