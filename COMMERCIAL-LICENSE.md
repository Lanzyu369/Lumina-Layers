# Lumina-Layers separate commercial authorization

## English

Individuals and businesses may use the software under [AGPLv3](LICENSE) free of charge. Commercial use alone does not require a paid software license. Selling generated models or physical prints with the required attribution is allowed under the project's [output rules](OUTPUT-TERMS.md).

The authorized development team offers two separate authorizations:

1. **Software commercial authorization:** permission to integrate Lumina-Layers into specified closed-source products without the AGPL obligations explicitly waived in the written agreement. The agreement identifies the covered code and versions, authorized parties, products, deployment and distribution scope, duration and fees. This applies to rights the development team is authorized to grant; independent and third-party components retain their own terms.
2. **Generated-output attribution waiver:** permission to sell or distribute specified generated outputs without Lumina-Layers attribution, within the parties, uses and period identified in the written agreement. This waiver does not also grant closed-source software integration rights or waive software-interface attribution or third-party obligations.

These authorizations may be purchased separately or together. Neither is granted automatically by this document, payment alone, or obtaining the source code. A separate written authorization from the development team must identify the rights granted. Contact the development team through the community contact channels in the README; pricing is agreed separately.

## 中文

个人和企业均可按 [AGPLv3](LICENSE) 免费使用软件，单纯商业使用不需要付费的软件授权。遵守项目 [生成物规则](OUTPUT-TERMS.md) 中的署名要求，可以销售生成模型及实体打印件。

有权开发组提供两类独立授权：

1. **软件商业授权**：允许在指定闭源产品中集成 Lumina-Layers，豁免书面协议明确列出的 AGPL 义务。协议应明确覆盖的代码和版本、被授权主体、产品、部署与分发范围、期限及费用。授权仅覆盖开发组有权授予的权利；独立组件和第三方组件继续适用其原条款。
2. **生成物署名豁免授权**：允许在书面协议明确的主体、用途和期限内，销售或分发指定生成物而无需标注 Lumina-Layers。该豁免不同时授予软件闭源集成权，也不豁免软件界面署名或第三方义务。

两项授权可以单独购买，也可以打包。本文件、单纯付款或取得源码均不自动授予这些权利；须由开发组另行书面明确授权范围。可通过 README 中的社区联系渠道联系开发组，价格另行约定。
