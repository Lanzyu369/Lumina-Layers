Version: 1.0. Applies starting with Lumina Studio 2.0.0.

## English

**By using this project's source code, you agree to these generated-output terms.**

These separate generated-output terms do not amend the AGPL license on the software.

### Scope

“Generated outputs” means model files generated with Lumina-Layers and physical prints made from those models.

### Conditions of use

Generated outputs may be used personally, commercially or shared publicly without a separate commercial authorization for those outputs. You must credit Lumina-Layers when publicly sharing or selling them, unless you have obtained an applicable attribution waiver. Personal noncommercial private use requires no attribution.

| Use | Conditions |
| --- | --- |
| Personal generation and printing solely for noncommercial private use | Free; no attribution required |
| Noncommercial online sharing of generated models | Free; must credit Lumina-Layers |
| Commercial sale of generated model files | Allowed; must credit Lumina-Layers |
| Commercial sale of physical prints of generated models | Allowed; must credit Lumina-Layers |
| Public sharing or sale without attribution | Must obtain a paid attribution waiver from the development team |

### Attribution

Use **Generated with Lumina-Layers** or **由 Lumina-Layers 生成**. The attribution must be clearly visible in at least one of the following: the model listing, product page, packaging or accompanying description. Engraving the physical print is not required.

### Attribution waiver

If you wish to share or sell generated outputs without attribution, you may purchase an attribution waiver from the development team. Its scope is defined by the team's separate written authorization. See [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md).

The waiver applies to generated outputs only. Software licensing and original-project attribution in the interface remain as described in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

### Copyright and input materials

The project does not claim copyright in users' generated outputs. You must obtain the necessary rights to input images, models and other materials for your intended use.

生成物允许个人自用、商业使用和公开分享，无须另行取得生成物商业授权。除已取得适用的署名豁免授权外，公开分享或销售生成物时，必须标注 Lumina-Layers。个人非商业自用无须标注。

| 使用行为 | 条件 |
| --- | --- |
| 个人生成、打印，仅供非商业自用 | 免费，无须标注 |
| 非商业在线分享生成的模型 | 免费，必须标注 Lumina-Layers |
| 商业销售生成的模型文件 | 允许，必须标注 Lumina-Layers |
| 商业销售生成模型的实体打印件 | 允许，必须标注 Lumina-Layers |
| 公开分享或销售生成物，但不愿标注 | 必须向开发组购买署名豁免授权 |

### 署名方式

署名使用 **由 Lumina-Layers 生成** 或 **Generated with Lumina-Layers**，应在模型发布说明、商品页面、包装或随附说明中至少一处清晰展示，无须刻在打印成品上。

### 署名豁免

如需免除公开分享或销售时的署名义务，可向开发组购买署名豁免授权，具体范围以开发组另行书面授权为准。详见 [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md)。

署名豁免仅适用于生成物。软件许可及界面原项目署名要求仍按 [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) 执行。
